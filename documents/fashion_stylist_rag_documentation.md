## SECTION 2: CRANIAL FRAMING & HAIR-SCULPTING TECHNIQUES 

Hair acts as a permanent structural accessory that alters perceived facial geometry across all styles and cuts.

* **The Butterfly Haircut & Long Layers (Feminine / Fluid):**
  * *Structural Mechanics:* Short face-framing layers starting near the chin combined with longer cascading layers below; graduated lengths starting from the collarbone.
  * *Styling Application:* Creates maximum volume, adds movement, and softens jawlines or wide foreheads.
* **Textured Crop & French Crop (Masculine / Short):**
  * *Structural Mechanics:* Short, textured top with faded or cropped sides, often paired with a blunt or textured fringe.
  * *Styling Application:* Adds height and visual structure, ideal for balancing round or oval facial lines.
* **Classic Quiff & Pompadour (Masculine / Medium):**
  * *Structural Mechanics:* Volume swept upward and backward from the forehead, tapering at the sides and back.
  * *Styling Application:* Elongates round or square facial structures by adding vertical dimension.

## SECTION 3: TONAL CHROMATICS & COLOR THEORY

### 3.1 Seasonal Color Analysis Framework (Universal)
* **Spring (Warm & Clear):** High-clarity, warm undertones. Optimized by clear, vibrant hues like peach, coral, warm yellow, turquoise, and camel.
* **Summer (Cool & Muted):** Low-contrast, cool undertones. Optimized by dusty, gentle shades like lavender, powder blue, slate gray, and cocoa.
* **Autumn (Warm & Earthy):** Rich, warm undertones. Optimized by deep grounded tones like olive green, rust, mustard, terracotta, and burnt orange.
* **Winter (Cool & Bright):** High-contrast, cool undertones. Optimized by stark, saturated shades like emerald green, true red, royal blue, jet black, and crisp white.

### 3.2 The 60-30-10 Distribution Rule
To maintain visual harmony in any outfit or collection:
1. **60% Dominant Tone:** Establishes the foundational color (outerwear, main suiting, trousers, or primary dress body).
2. **30% Secondary Tone:** Supports the foundation and adds depth (blazers, cardigans, secondary layering pieces).
3. **10% Accent Tone:** Introduces contrast and focal points (ties, pocket squares, scarves, handbags, statement jewelry, footwear details).

## SECTION 4: WARDROBE CURATION & CAPSULE FRAMEWORKS

### 4.1 Feminine Capsule Essentials
* **Foundational Tops:** Crisp white button-down, neutral silk camisole, core cotton crewnecks (black/white/gray).
* **Structural Bottoms:** Tailored high-waisted trousers, dark-wash straight-leg denim, structured midi skirt.
* **Outer Layers:** Structured wool trench, tailored blazer, classic leather jacket.
* **Footwear Core:** Nude block heels, minimalist white leather sneakers, sleek ankle boots.

### 4.2 Masculine Capsule Essentials
* **Foundational Tops:** Crisp Oxford cotton button-down shirts (white/light blue), premium fitted crewneck tees (black, white, navy).
* **Structural Bottoms:** Tailored slim-fit chinos, dark-wash selvedge denim jeans, pleated or flat-front dress trousers.
* **Outer Layers:** Structured double-breasted overcoat, tailored two-piece suit jacket, minimal bomber jacket or denim trucker jacket.
* **Footwear Core:** Classic leather dress shoes (oxfords/derbies), clean white leather court sneakers, suede Chelsea boots.
